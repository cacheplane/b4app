## 2026-11-13: three CI flakes

Three tests that failed on unrelated pull requests and on `main`, each traced to its cause from CI's logs and results. Branch `blove/ci-flakes`, from `main` f8a28e0.

### The package tests' stall (`MirrorSyncTests`)

- Seen: seven `MirrorSyncTests` waits ("first page", "parked in its long poll", "snapshot applied") timed out together at 79.5 s on #191 and #193, and twice on the palette branch. Each waits at most 60 s.
- What the logs show: in #193's two attempts the first test of 1,791 finished after about 50 s (the passing attempt) and 70 s (the failing one); in the passing attempt the same seven took 58 to 62 s, just under their limit, and in the failing one their 60 s timers fired 19 s late. Whatever held the process held every thread, timers included.
- A first diagnosis was wrong for CI. Locally (10 cores) the same stall came from starting the shared cluster: `sample` showed nine cooperative threads waiting on `ensureStarted`'s lock and the tenth in `sweepStale` (old clusters in `$TMPDIR`) and then `initdb`, and a trivial mirror test took 69.6 s. On CI the cluster, started before the run, was "in use after 0.0 s", and still the first test finished after 48.9 s.
- Cause on CI, from `sample` of the test process in CI (three cooperative threads, so three cores), 22 s into the run: all three threads busy for the whole 2 s sample, and in 3,893 of their 4,050 samples waiting in `poll` (libpq waiting on the server), 2,761 of them under `EphemeralPostgres.endpoint()`'s `CREATE DATABASE` and most of the rest in migrations (`WorkspaceDatabase.init`, `Migrator.migrate`). The storage and coordinator tests (511 of 1,851) each make a database first, synchronously, from a cooperative thread; at the start of the run they hold every thread while the server works, so nothing else in the process runs.
- Change: CI runs `WorkspaceStorageTests` and `CoordinatorTests` in one `swift test` process and everything else in another (one after the other: SwiftPM's lock on `.build` keeps two from running at once), so the rest have threads of their own. Both use one cluster: with `B4APP_TEST_POSTGRES_DIR` set, the cluster lives in that directory and outlives the process, and a process that finds it running uses it at once; CI starts it with one test first. The sweep of earlier runs' clusters runs on its own thread, never under the lock, and the start prints how long it took to stderr.
- Not done: the database calls stay synchronous on cooperative threads (140 of the 162 calls are in synchronous tests). In one process, on a machine with few cores, the stall is still there.
- Checked locally: the same eight suites, before and after the sweep change: `MirrorSyncTests` 71.0 s and the run 108.6 s, then 8.2 s and 47.9 s (start 1.7 s; earlier runs had already swept the old clusters) and 8.3 s and 48.8 s on a warmed cluster. A directory whose socket path would pass 104 bytes is refused by name. Turning `fsync` off did not speed the database suites here (192.7 s with it, 274.5 s without, on a Mac busy with other checkouts' builds), so the cluster's settings are unchanged.

### The palette's drag (`PaletteSmokeTests.testPalettePanelDragsSnapsAndKeepsItsShadow`)

Failed four times (twice on `main`), in two ways.

- 12 pt short (main at 00:32): the press's own trace showed the last drag event 12 pt before the release; the panel moved on drag events only, so it ended one step short. `PalettePanel` now takes the release as the drag's last step (and a release past the threshold with no drag event is a drag). Unit test `aDragEndsWhereThePointerIsReleased`: failed at 12.0 pt, passes. #198 (D-225) made the same change on `main` meanwhile; the merge keeps `main`'s code and test.
- Into the Dock (main at 19:42): the screen recording shows no window at launch; the test checked once, without waiting, and opened one with File > New Window. That window never showed the command centre, so the palette opened at its fallback place, 140 pt lower, and the 130 pt drag down was clamped against the Dock (286 + 422 = 708, the Dock's top). Two causes: `CommandCenterPresenter` attached its panel only from a SwiftUI update, and the first came before the view had a window; a window opened while the workspace already shows (New Window here, a window reopened from the Dock in the app) brings no later update. The presenter's view now attaches when it moves to a window. Unit test `theButtonComesWithAWindowThatArrivesLater`: failed (no panel), passes. The smoke tests now wait up to 15 s for the launch window before New Window (`launchWithWindow`, in all six places).

### The banner journey's rotation (`BannerLayoutTests.testConnectionNoticeJourney`)

- Seen once on `main`: "Failed to set device orientation: Timed out waiting for confirmation of orientation change", from `launch()`'s portrait reset in the run's first test, straight after the Simulator booted; the confirmation came 15 s later.
- Change: `launch()` turns the device upright only when it is in landscape (#198 on `main` made it skip a device already upright; this keeps the narrower test, which also skips a Simulator that reports no orientation yet); the landscape journey turns it back in a teardown block too, so a failed step cannot leave it on its side.
