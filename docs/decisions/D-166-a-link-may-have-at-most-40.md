## 2026-11-03 (draining per user, commands in flight, a quiet listener)

- **D-166 A link may have at most 40 commands running at once.** The read loop never waits, and every command ran on a task of its own, claimed before the rate limit and able to wait 5 s to deliver even a refusal, so a device that sent faster than it read could grow tasks without bound. Each link now holds a semaphore of `COMMANDS_IN_FLIGHT` (twice `COMMAND_BURST`) permits; a command without one is answered `rate_limited` (`retryAfterMs: 1000`) inline, uncounted, unless it is a resend of one still in flight, which attaches as before. Phoenix bounds its per-transport channels the same way (`max_channels_per_transport`). Test seam: `Relay::set_commands_in_flight_max`.
  - **Tests.** `instances.rs`: a draining instance frees a user whose link closed while another user's unread link keeps the drain going, and `b` serves them at once; a deafened listener reconnects and wakes a 20 s poll within 5 s. `relay.rs`: with a cap of 2, a third command is refused at once, a resend attaches, and an answer frees room. Each failed with its change undone.
