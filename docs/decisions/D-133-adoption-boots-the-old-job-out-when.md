## 2026-11-02 (sign-in polish from the live gate)

- **D-133 Adoption boots the old job out when `launchctl print` cannot tell, and again after the move.** Amends D-094 and D-116. `LegacyAdoption.jobState`, `bootOutDecision(state:…)`, `bootOutLegacyJob`, `Production.retireLegacyAgent` (`Sources/Coordinator/Accounts/LegacyAdoption.swift`). At the live gate a `launchctl print` that timed out read as "not loaded", the boot-out was skipped, and `com.cacheplane.b4app.coordinator` stayed loaded until a manual `launchctl bootout`. Now the print's answer is one of three: exit 0 is loaded, exit 113 ("Could not find service") is not loaded, and anything else (a timeout, another code, a launchctl that would not run) is unknown, with the print's timeout raised from 5 to 15 s. An unknown job is booted out by D-052's rule when its plist names a program other than this coordinator; with no plist, or this binary's, it is skipped as before rather than refused, since nothing shows it is loaded. A boot-out of an unknown job that launchctl answers with 3 ("No such process") or 113 counts as not loaded; any other failure still stops adoption with nothing moved. After the move, `retireLegacyAgent` runs the same boot-out before it removes the plist, so a job that came back, or that the first look could not see, is gone too; a failure there is logged and the plist still goes. Only launchd stops the job's process; the coordinator signals nothing, and the lock and cluster checks of D-094 are unchanged.
