## 2026-11-15 (a running turn says what it is doing)

- **D-239 A running turn ends in a line that says what it is doing: at normal density always, and at dense where the turn has nothing to fold and no reply yet (amends D-223).** The hill climb's audit found that normal density had no line saying what is running, and that neither density drew anything between a send and the turn's first item (its defect 7): after the message, the thread was still until the runtime's first item came.
  - **The line.** `DenseTranscript.Entry.running(RunningLine)` carries the turn, whether it waits for the user, and the newest in-progress counted step's name; its words are the ones a dense steps line draws while its turn runs (D-223): "Waiting for you", else the name (first line, cut at 120 characters), else "Working". Running is the four statuses D-223 named: submitting, running, waiting for the user, stopping. A turn that ended, one whose outcome is unknown, and history have no line.
  - **Normal density.** `DenseTranscript.plain` puts the line after the running turn's last item, where an ended turn's note stands; a running turn with no items yet (begun on another device, its message not here yet) has it after everything. It stays while a reply streams: the reply's own text shows that it is streaming, and the line still says which step is open.
  - **Dense.** A running turn's steps line already says what is running once there is something to fold, and a streaming reply is read as it comes. The running line is drawn only where neither exists: before the turn's first item. D-223's rule that a turn with only its answer has no steps line is unchanged; `aRunningTurnWithNothingYetHasNoLine` is now `aRunningTurnWithNothingYetHasNoStepsLine` and expects the running line before the first item.
  - **How it is drawn.** `RunningLineRow`: the working mark (`ThreadMark`, whose arc turns and is still under Reduce Motion) and the words in the muted small face, one line cut at its tail, the height of a steps line. A turn waiting for the user has the words and no mark, since the request below it is what asks. It is not a control; VoiceOver reads its words. It is a row of the transcript, so it counts in the 150-row window (D-237) and follows the scroll rules as any row does.
  - **Not done.** The line is not announced when it changes (the audit's defect 9); "Stopping" is not a word of its own; the gap between pressing Send and the turn existing is still the pending message row.
  - **Verification.** Five cases in `PlainTranscriptTests` and two in `DenseTranscriptTests`, written first and failing to compile before the entry existed; two ledgers of `TranscriptCorpusTests` (F09 and the stopped-beside-running case) now list the line at normal density.
