## 2026-11-12 (place palette)

- **D-209 Where a new thread starts is chosen in a palette on the Mac and a sheet on the phone; the picker row loses its chevrons.** This builds "Starting a thread" from the thread list and environments design (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-thread-list-and-environments-design.md`, mockups `palette-v18` and `composer-v15`), the step its build order calls "composer palette". The place picker was a system menu (D-183); the owner asked for the palette the design chose.
  - **The Mac.** The place opens `PlacePaletteView` over the new-thread view: the view is blurred 8 pt under the window colour at 60%, fading in over .18 s; the palette is 420 wide, radius 14, a hairline edge and the popover shadow, rising 10 pt from .98 scale over .2 s and leaving in .12 s. A "Start in…" filter (40 high, 15 pt, focused on open); 36 pt rows, the name at 14 pt and the path in JetBrains Mono 11 with the home folder as "~"; one highlight that slides (.12 s) after the pointer and Up and Down and scrolls into view; about five rows before the list scrolls; "Nothing matches. Choose a folder below." when the filter matches nothing. A 48 pt footer on the surface colour: "Choose folder… ⌘O" (the system folder panel; off on a Mac that relays through the hub, as the menu item was hidden) and "No folder", equal bordered buttons, and "esc to close". Return chooses, Escape or a click on the veil closes, and the label's new text fades up over .18 s.
  - **The rows.** One per environment, then, for an environment with several folders, one indented row per folder. The environment's own row starts in its first folder; a folder row in that folder. The filter matches names and paths, ignoring case: an environment whose name matches keeps all its folders, otherwise only matching folders are listed under it. The check is on the environment's row for one folder and on the folder's row for several. The highlight opens on the checked row, else the first. `PlacePalette` holds this apart from the views.
  - **The phone.** The place opens a "Start in" sheet (medium and large detents): the same rows without paths, then "No folder". No "Choose folder…", as the design says.
  - **The row and the heading.** The three pickers are plain muted words with no chevrons; the live ones take the raised fill and ink text on hover. The words are 12 pt on the Mac (the design's size) and stay at the phone's 15 pt, which the iOS layout tests align with the field. The heading reads "Let's get started", centred over the field at regular widths and leading on a compact width, where the field sits at the bottom.
  - **Builder's calls.** ⌘O works while the palette is open, not anywhere in the new-thread view. Choosing "Choose folder…" closes the palette before the panel opens. The palette is hosted by the new-thread view, so the sidebar is never veiled.
  - **Left for later.** The environment form, the design's last step, is not part of this.
