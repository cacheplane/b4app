## 2026-11-06 (thread recovery notices)

- **D-190 Archived native sessions retain their recovery guidance, and one bounded notice explains each thread failure.** Amends D-036's error and continuity presentation; applies D-047 to expanded diagnostics.
  - **Classification and recovery.** An archived resume is `protocol_invalid` with `reason: native_session_archived`, its native diagnostic, and the existing unarchive command. Only a rejection identifying the requested thread's missing history becomes `native_session_missing`; unrelated errors retain their cause. Deliberate checks surface failures, while ordinary cache reads remain offline-safe. A confirmed archived response repairs an obsolete missing classification; it neither unarchives the session nor sends a message.
  - **Draft ownership.** A rejected archived send may restore its draft only while its original thread is still selected and open, its account generation is unchanged, and the thread has not been forgotten. Late responses cannot restore text into another chat, a deleted chat, or a signed-out workspace.
  - **Presentation.** A persistent continuity notice replaces only its matching transient error. The explanation uses the card's width, recovery and Dismiss controls share a row beneath it, and controls stack when needed. Technical diagnostics stay behind Details in a selectable scroll region capped at 120 points; their length must not determine the split view's minimum height.
