## 2026-11-06 (iPhone widget background catch-up)

- **D-188 Background wakes finish a fresh feed catch-up, not the next batch (amends D-127, D-147).** A push wake previously waited for any `AppliedBatch` before parking sync. A partial page or snapshot could release that wait while a newly notified thread was still beyond it; an empty feed produced no batch at all. `MirrorSync.refresh(waitingAtMost:)` registers a generation before nudging and completes only after a cycle begun after that request has applied and published a final `more=false` page. Pending refreshes and remaining pages disable long polling independently of `nudged`, which is cleared at cycle start. Cancellation, timeout, stop and authorization failure release waiters once. The account remains responsible for resuming and parking its loop.
  - **Overlapping wakes.** `MirrorRefreshCoordinator` tracks every host wake, including one arriving during reconciliation or parking, and repeats the whole cycle if another arrived. All callers share the first wake's eleven-second overall budget (three for a queued resume plus eight for catch-up); an unserviced late wake cannot inherit an older read's success. Clearing the active task is atomic with the final generation check. The host retains the final widget reload, notification reconciliation and ordered park before its system completion callback. The helper is Foundation-only and fixture-tested, with no UIKit in `WorkspaceMirror`.
  - **Evidence and limits.** The owner's H alert and badge arrived with Low Power Mode off but its widget row appeared only after foregrounding; a later background dot update and I's locked-phone reply withdrawal passed. The fixture reproduces the first-page bug using the real sync, file-backed mirror and widget reader; it does not prove which step was delayed on the owner's iPhone. Safe background/timeline diagnostics help distinguish future scheduling from catch-up failure. No schema, wire, entitlement or minimum-OS change; background execution and WidgetKit scheduling remain controlled by iOS. Verification: `docs/verification/2026-11-06-widget-background-catchup.md`.
