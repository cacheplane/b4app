## 2026-11-20 (the Mac's link after a coordinator restart)

- **D-265 When the coordinator's connection closes under a ready link, the app connects again by itself: after 250 ms, then 1 s, 2 s, and every 5 s until a connect is ready. Retry stays.** The coordinator restarts on `agent-workspace coordinator restart`, on an update, and when it exits; its socket closes and the app's event stream ends. The app then showed "Coordinator unavailable: Coordinator connection closed." and did nothing more until Retry was pressed, so a thread made meanwhile (from the CLI, or synced from the hub) and every later event never reached the list. Seen on 2026-11-10 (`docs/verification/2026-11-10-no-folder-starts.md`) and on 2026-11-19, where it was first taken for "a thread made from the CLI is not listed until it has a turn".
  - **The rule.** `WorkspaceModel.observe` ends with the link unavailable only when its client is still the current one, as before, and now starts `reconnectAfterClose`: it waits each of `reconnectDelays` in turn on the model's clock, calls `connect()` when the link is unavailable, and stops once a connect is ready. A Retry pressed meanwhile is an ordinary connect; the attempts end when the link is ready. `disconnect()` cancels them. A first connect that fails is unchanged: it shows the notice and waits for Retry.
  - **Why these waits (the writer's call).** The first is short so a quick restart is hardly seen; the Mac's transport already waits up to 10 s for the launch agent's coordinator once its socket is gone, so an attempt costs one socket connect while the coordinator is up and a bounded wait while it is down. The restart's own length was not measured.
  - **The phone.** It shares the model; its transport is the mirror, which does not close under a ready link in ordinary use. Not checked on the phone.
  - **Left out.** A different notice while attempts run ("Reconnecting…"); counting or capping the attempts.
  - **Tests.** `LinkReconnectTests` (`aClosedLinkConnectsAgainByItself`, `aFailedAttemptTriesAgainLater`, `disconnectingStopsTheAttempts`), with a fake transport per connect and the test clock.
