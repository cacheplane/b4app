## 2026-11-02 (M8f, attention on the phone)

- **D-129 The TestFlight export carries the app's entitlements.** `Scripts/upload-ios.sh`, `Scripts/ios-entitlements.py`. The archive stays unsigned (an automatically signed archive needs a development profile, which needs a registered device), and Xcode's export can only keep entitlements the archive has, so before this the exported app and widget were signed with the four basic entitlements only: no App Group, push, Sign in with Apple, or associated domains, although the App Store profiles allowed them. The script now resolves each target's `CODE_SIGN_ENTITLEMENTS` against its build settings for the flavour's configuration, signs the archived widget and then the app ad hoc with them (`xcodebuild` refuses ad hoc signing for iOS 26.5 device builds; `codesign` does not), and after the export checks that every resolved entitlement is in the signature (`aps-environment` as `production`), failing before validation otherwise. Found while regenerating the iPhone profiles for M8f.
