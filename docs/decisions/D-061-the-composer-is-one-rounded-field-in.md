## 2026-11-01 (composer)

- **D-061 The composer is one rounded field in two shapes, with a round send button, and grows to three lines.** At the owner's direction ("behave like the input for iMessage, or ChatGPT"; chose the ChatGPT-style box from three mockups, "responsive to a single line on larger devices"; "be diligent and precise" about spacing and alignment). It replaces D-036's composer card with its hint row and Send/Stop pills.
  - Shapes: the box (every iPhone, and an iPad or Mac once the draft wraps or has a line break) holds the text on top and a tool row underneath with send at its trailing end; the line (iPad and Mac while the draft fits beside the button, and never at an accessibility text size) is a 44 pt capsule with send inline. `ComposerView.shape` decides from the field's width and the draft's natural width, so the shape cannot oscillate; one `AnyLayout` holds the same children in both shapes, so the text keeps focus when the shape changes.
  - Metrics: a 44 pt line and a 32 pt button, which sits 6 pt from every edge it touches, so it is concentric with the field's 22 pt corner. Corners are circular, not continuous: a continuous corner at half the height flattens the capsule's ends, and only a circular arc is concentric with the button. Text starts 16 pt in, and its first line is centred in the top 44 pt in both shapes, so nothing jumps when the shape changes; 4 pt between the text and the tool row. The line, the button, and the gaps grow with Dynamic Type up to 1.5 times (a 48 pt button at AX3). Measured in the Simulator and the Mac snapshots (`docs/verification.md`).
  - Text: Hanken Grotesk 16 pt relative to `.callout` on the phone (a step under the 17 pt replies, at the owner's "too large and strong"), the Mac's 15 pt body there; ink text, muted "Message <connection>" prompt, cobalt caret. It grows a line at a time to three, then scrolls inside. The phone uses a vertical `TextField` (no inner inset, so the text sits exactly on the grid; Return adds a line, the button sends); the Mac keeps `TextEditor`, whose Return the send button's shortcut takes and whose Shift-Return adds a line.
  - Send and stop: one round button in the same place, ink with the background-coloured arrow; while a turn runs it is a stop square, as in ChatGPT. Disabled, it is a raised-surface circle with a ghost glyph rather than faded ink. A round icon button departs from D-036's worded pills; the arrow and the square are the controls' meaning, not decoration, and each keeps its accessible name ("Send", "Stop") and a 44 pt target. The turn's state and, when its outcome is unknown, Check again and Abandon turn sit above the field, the words aligned with the text inside it (two lines at most on the Mac, whose smallest window must fit the detail).
  - On the phone the transcript scrolls on under the composer (`safeAreaBar`, with the system's soft scroll edge) instead of being cut off above a rule; the Mac keeps the rule.
  - Room for attachments: a turn carries text only today (Codex accepts images, Claude Code images and documents, B4 text). When it can carry more, a row of attachment previews goes between the text and the tool row, a non-empty row forces the box, and attach takes the tool row's leading end. Neither is drawn until something can be sent, so no control does nothing.
