## 2026-11-04 (a 429 at the link upgrade)

The last low-priority finding from reading Phoenix's source against the hub (`docs/research/2026-11-03-phoenix-source-hardening/socket_layer.md`, L6). Numbered D-177 by `Scripts/docs-index.sh --next`.

- **D-177 A device's relay link waits out a `429`'s `Retry-After` before it upgrades again.** The hub answers an upgrade over a device's limit (10 a minute) `429 rate_limited` with `Retry-After` in whole seconds (`Hub/crates/b4hub/src/error.rs`), but `URLSessionHubLinkConnection` turned every refusal into `HubLinkError.refused(status:)` and `RelayLink` retried a 429 on its ordinary backoff, from 1 s, so a device over the limit kept knocking and kept being refused. A 429 is now `HubLinkError.rateLimited(retryAfter:)` with the header parsed, and `RelayLink` waits the larger of its backoff and the `Retry-After`, capped at `maxRetryAfter` (120 s) so a hub cannot park a device for long. Both the Mac's `HubLink` and the phone's `MirrorRelay` build on `RelayLink`, so both get it. Tests: `RelayLinkTests.rateLimitedUpgradeWaitsOutItsRetryAfter` (a 7 s wait, and the cap), and `URLSessionLinkTests.aRateLimitedUpgradeCarriesItsRetryAfter` (a real 429 from the loopback hub, its header read).
