## 2026-11-02 (sign-in build)

- **D-108 The iPhone's Log out ends the session and keeps the device; amends D-053.** `MirrorAccount.logOut`, `MirrorLogOut` (`Sources/WorkspaceMirror`). Log out stops the sync and waits for its loop, ends the session (`DELETE /v1/sessions/self`, at most 10 s), forgets the token, empties the mirror, and returns to the sign-in screen. The device is remembered in `device.json` beside the mirror (up to 8 per hub, most recent first), so a later passkey or Apple sign-in reuses it. A `401` from the sync or from an account call signs the phone out the same way, without the hub call, and empties the mirror too, so the next account never sees the last one's rows; the sign-in screen then says "This iPhone was signed out of <org>." A phone that rejoins by an enrollment code or a pairing scan (the merge-first path included) offers the same remembered devices, so it keeps its device too (D-096, amended at the live gate). A typed code needs a signed-out phone: "Use an enrollment code" is only on the sign-in screen, and `MirrorAccount.enroll` refuses while a link is stored.
