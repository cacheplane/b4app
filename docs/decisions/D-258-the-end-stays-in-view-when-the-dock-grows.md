## 2026-11-19 (the transcript when what is docked under it grows)

- **D-258 A reader at the end of a thread still sees the end when what is docked under the transcript gets taller. The request card stays docked.** The request cards, the continuity banner, an error and the composer are docked under the transcript, so each takes its height from the transcript's viewport. On screen in the Local build from main (2d5395f7), a Claude Code approval card arrived and the message just sent, with the last four lines of the reply above it, went out of sight under the card; an error banner and a draft that wrapped to three lines did the same and drew "Jump to latest". The transcript kept its end in view when its content changed size (`defaultScrollAnchor(.bottom, for: .sizeChanges)`) and when rows or stream text arrived, and did nothing when its viewport got shorter.
  - **The owner's choice.** Three placements were drawn: the card docked as now with the end kept in view, the card as the turn's last row in the transcript, and the card in place of the composer while the turn waits. The owner chose the first. It is also the only one that covers the error banner and a growing draft.
  - **The rule.** When the transcript's viewport gets shorter and nothing holds the view, `TranscriptView` goes back to the end. A reader who scrolled back, or who holds a turn open, stays where they are. The layout pass that shortens the viewport moves the view down and up again before it rests; that upward move is not read as the reader scrolling back.
  - **Tests.** `TranscriptScrollTests.theEndStaysInViewWhenTheDockGrows` (hosted in a window; failed before the change, 160 pt short of the end) and `aGrowingDockLeavesAReaderWhoScrolledBack`.
