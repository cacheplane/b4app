## 2026-11-01 (iPhone fit)

- **D-060 The iPhone draws the shared SwiftUI at iOS sizes: its own type scale, 44 pt targets, 16 pt margins, and layouts that stack at accessibility text sizes.** At the owner's direction ("more mobile friendly; refer to Apple's HIG"), from the research in `docs/research/reports/iOS HIG mobile layout guidance.md`. D-036's identity is unchanged (ink, white, cobalt, Hanken Grotesk and JetBrains Mono, one accent); only sizes and metrics move, and every change is behind `#if os(iOS)`, a platform metric, or a Dynamic Type check, so the Mac draws as before (`SnapshotTests`, by eye).
  - Type: `LLATypeScale` holds two scales. The Mac keeps D-036's (body 15, rows 13). The phone starts each role at its iOS text style's default size, so the custom faces read like the system's and `relativeTo:` scales them in step: body, rows, and buttons 17 (`.body`), small 15 (`.subheadline`), caption 12, title 20 (`.title3`), display 28 (`.title`), code 15 and code-small 13 (JetBrains Mono's taller x-height reads 15 pt as Hanken's 17). Nothing is under the HIG's 11 pt floor. With Bold Text on, regular text takes the semibold face, since SwiftUI does not embolden a custom font. Text inside a shape that already scales (an avatar's initials, the B4 mark) uses `LLAFont.sans(fixed:)`.
  - Targets: `LLA.Metrics.row` is 44 on iOS and 0 on the Mac. Sidebar action rows, thread rows, profile-menu items, search results, and the profile button are at least that tall and tappable across their width; the lists set `defaultMinListRowHeight` to it so there is no dead band between rows (before: 28 pt targets on a 52 pt pitch). Link buttons ("Dismiss", "Retry") get a 44 pt tall target on the phone; a transcript card's Show/Hide target reaches over the card's padding (`contentShape` inset), so the closed card is the button without growing.
  - Margins: `LLA.Metrics.margin` is the iPhone's 16 pt system margin (24 on the Mac) for the transcript, composer, header, Settings pages, and Connect.
  - Thread detail: the inline navigation title is the only title (the in-content 26 pt display title was a second copy under the large title); the header keeps the subtitle and the badges. Rename, Pin, and Archive move into one "…" menu in the bar so the title keeps its width (HIG Toolbars: the essentials in the bar, the rest in a More menu); the Mac keeps three buttons. The composer puts Send beside the editor, as in Messages, and drops the "Enter sends; Shift-Enter" hint, which a phone without a keyboard cannot use; dragging the transcript dismisses the keyboard.
  - Accessibility sizes (`isAccessibilitySize`): titles wrap instead of truncating (thread rows to three lines, sidebar rows to two); a transcript card's title takes the width with its badge and Show/Hide below; the detail header, a connection card's heading and sign-in line, and the composer's buttons stack; `LLAFacts`/`LLAFact` turn Diagnostics' and a runtime's two-column grids into label-above-value. Symbol columns, the attention dot, runtime marks, and the avatar grow with `@ScaledMetric`; badges never wrap.
  - Design review (ui-ux-pro-max's app checklist plus D-036 and the LLA design rules, same day), fixed here: the New thread sheet was clipped on the iPhone by its 480 pt minimum width, tinted system blue, and defaulted to the phone's sandbox folder; on the phone it is now a form sheet with Cancel and Create in the bar, the theme's tint, no "Choose…" (the phone's picker sees the phone's files), and the newest thread's folder as the default. Navigation-bar titles (a pushed thread, Search, Settings and its pages, New thread) draw in Hanken Grotesk SemiBold through the bar's appearance proxy instead of SF Pro. The composer shows "Message <connection>" when empty instead of a blank box. A pinned row's pin moves to the trailing edge beside the runtime mark, so every title starts on the same edge. Rows (sidebar, thread, profile menu, search) fill `surfaceRaised` while pressed (`LLARowButtonStyle`) so a tap answers at once. Search results drop the separators and take the sidebar's row insets. The thread subtitle on the phone reads "Codex · b4app · on <Mac>": the kind only when the label does not say it, and the folder's name rather than a path cut mid-word. Assistant replies are trimmed of trailing blank lines, which made the gaps between items uneven. A connection card puts the account on its own line under "Signed in as" rather than wrapping "Signed in as: authenticated" beside it. Settings lists surface rows on the page background instead of the system's grouped greys. Tried and dropped: section titles in More's menu (iOS drops a `Section` title around an inline picker in a menu).
  - Follow-ups (same day): the phone's Settings pages are read-only. The mirror refuses managing connections, their sign-in, and local servers, and M8c keeps that on the Mac (its design's non-goals), so on the phone Connections drops "Add a connection", Check, Sign in, and Re-check runtimes, and Local servers drops Add a project, Start, Stop, Restart, Inspect routes, Connect, and the log; each page says once that these are managed on the Mac (`WorkspaceDevice` in `SettingsView.swift`). The Hanken navigation titles now follow the text size while the app runs: `LLANavigationBar` recomputes them on `UIContentSizeCategory.didChangeNotification` for the appearance proxy and for the bars already on screen, and measures against the app's current size explicitly (without that, `scaledFont(for:)` measured against the default size, so the titles did not grow even when the app launched at a large size). The inline title stops growing at the largest non-accessibility size, since the bar's height is fixed; the large title keeps growing (my call).
  - Not done, and left for the owner: the HIG prefers toolbar actions to action rows in an iPhone list (Mail's bottom bar: filter, search, compose), which would undo D-053's New thread / Search / More rows on the phone; and the phone's Connections page still offers "Add … connection", which only the Mac can carry out.
