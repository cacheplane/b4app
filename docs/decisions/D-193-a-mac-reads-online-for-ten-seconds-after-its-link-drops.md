## 2026-11-09 (Mac presence through a blip)

The last open finding from reading Phoenix's source against the hub (`docs/research/2026-11-03-phoenix-source-hardening/pubsub_presence.md`, L5, a product call the owner made on 2026-11-09). Numbered D-193 by `Scripts/docs-index.sh --next` (cacheplane/b4app#148 holds D-192).

- **D-193 A Mac whose link drops reads online in `presence` for 10 seconds more, and its phones hear it went offline only if it is still gone then.** A Mac's network change, a short sleep, or a hub deploy (D-172) dropped its link and the hub told its phones it was offline at once, then online a second or two later, so the phone's Mac indicator flickered. Phoenix.Tracker waits out a `down_period` before it declares a node gone. Now `serve_link` unregisters a Mac's link *lingering* (`Relay::unregister_lingering`): the Mac goes into a lingering set before its link is removed, `presence` counts a lingering Mac online, and a task broadcasts presence after `OFFLINE_GRACE` (10 s) only if that link is still the one lingering and the Mac has not linked again. Ten seconds covers `RelayLink`'s first retries (1, 2, then 4 s with full jitter). A link the hub closed `4001` (revoked, or its session ended) does not linger: that Mac is not coming back. A link task that ends early (D-192) lingers.
  - **Scope.** Presence only. The registry still says the Mac has no link, so a command to it answers `device_offline` or is held at once, as before; the phone already words that answer. A phone that links during the grace is told the Mac is online. The cost is that a Mac that really left (its lid closed) reads online for 10 s longer.
  - **Tests.** `relay.rs`: `a_mac_that_links_again_within_the_grace_never_reads_offline`, `a_mac_still_gone_after_the_grace_reads_offline` (a phone linking within the grace sees it online; both phones hear offline after it), and `a_mac_whose_session_ended_reads_offline_at_once`. The first two fail with lingering switched off. The test harness sets the grace to zero (`Relay::set_offline_grace`, a test seam) so the other tests hear a Mac leave at once.
