## 2026-11-09 (hosted-hub operations: the first week's alarm noise and a backup gap)

- **D-196 The latency alarm measures the hub's own request latency with the long-poll and WebSocket paths excluded, and the RDS maintenance window sits three hours after the AWS Backup run. Amends D-071.** A week of running the hubs showed two faults in the monitoring as designed. The `latency-p99` alarm on the load balancer's `TargetResponseTime` was in ALARM or flapping from 2026-11-02 onward in both accounts: the sync long-poll (`GET /v1/changes`, up to 25 s) and the relay's WebSocket upgrade are most of the traffic with a few devices connected, so the p99 sat at 25 s and the alarm said nothing about the hub. And every Sunday's AWS Backup job failed in both accounts ("could not start because it is either inside or too close to the weekly maintenance window"): the plan runs at 06:00 UTC and the RDS maintenance window was `sun:06:30-sun:07:30`.
  - **Latency.** A CloudWatch Logs metric filter on the hub's log group publishes `elapsed_ms` of every `request complete` line whose `span.path` is neither `/v1/changes` nor `/v1/devices/self/link` as `RequestLatencyMs` in the namespace `b4hub/b4hub-<env>`; the alarm is p99 of that metric above 2000 ms for ten one-minute periods. The ALB metric stays available in the console for what it measures.
  - **Windows.** The RDS maintenance window moves to `sun:09:00-sun:10:00` UTC; the backup window (`05:00-06:00`) and the AWS Backup schedule (`cron(0 6 * * ? *)`) are unchanged. The copy-failure rule of D-194 would have paged for the missing copy had it existed; the backup-job failure itself is visible only in AWS Backup's job list, which the monthly drill reviews.
  - **Region policy, narrowed.** The `us-west-2` exception of the Organization's region policy, widened to `rds:*` and `ec2:*` during the restore drills (D-072 notes), is narrowed on 2026-11-09 to the listed RDS snapshot, instance, and subnet-group actions the copy and runbook 8 B use, and the EC2 `Describe*` calls a restore needs; `backup`, `backup-storage`, `kms`, and `iam:PassRole` stay. The previous policy is kept beside the earlier one under `~/.b4app/aws-org/`.
