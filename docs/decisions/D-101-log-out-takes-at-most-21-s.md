## 2026-11-02 (sign-in build)

- **D-101 Log out takes at most 21 s, and a session it left open on the hub is reported until dismissed.** `LogoutTimeouts.standard` in `Sources/Coordinator/Accounts/AccountCommands.swift`. With work running and `stopWork` false it answers `work_running` with the list and changes nothing. Otherwise: 9.5 s from the request's arrival (a lock wait of up to 3 s included) to interrupt local turns, stop owned servers, and dispose the runtimes side by side, with 3 s (or a third of a shorter step) kept for the server stops; 9.5 s to push the change log once; what is left of that, and at least 0.5 s, for `DELETE /v1/sessions/self` (a `401` counts as ended); then the token is forgotten and the registry row written before the scope closes, within 1.5 s, the rest finishing in the background. Each bound returns even from work that ignores cancellation (`Bounded.run`), and every adapter's `dispose()` is final. A session the hub did not end sets `hub_session_left_open`; `account.status` and `hub.status` carry "The last sign-out could not reach the hub; that session was left open there." until `account.dismissNotice` clears it, because the app polls `hub.status` and the design's "reported once" has to mean seen once. `agent-workspace hub status` prints the notice and dismisses it. Signing in to another account ends the previous one's session too, within 3 s, recorded the same way. One socket connection answers its commands in order, so the app saves the draft and logs out on a connection of its own (connect only, a 5 s hello, never spawning a coordinator); a log-out sent on a connection that is already waiting for a slow command (a `server.start`, up to 90 s) still waits for it.
