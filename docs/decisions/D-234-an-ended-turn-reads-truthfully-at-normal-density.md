## 2026-11-14 (an ended turn at normal density)

- **D-234 At normal density a step left open in a turn that is not running never reads as in progress, a step's badge is a word, and a turn's note stands with its turn (amends D-223).** No adapter and nothing in the coordinator closes a tool item that is still `inProgress` when its turn is stopped, fails or loses its runtime, so the stored row says `inProgress` for good. D-223's call 5 settled that for the dense line only. Normal density drew the stored word in the accent colour, and drew every "Turn stopped." and "Turn failed." after the last item of the thread, under whichever turn was last. The code is commit 99e913b4.
  - **One settle for both densities.** `DenseTranscript.settled(_:in:)` replaces dense's `stopped`: in a turn that has ended, or that the list does not know, an open step is `stopped`; in a turn whose outcome is unknown (`reconciling`) it is `unknown`; in a running turn it is left alone. The stored item is untouched, as before. D-223 left a reconciling turn's step `inProgress`; "unknown" is what is known.
  - **The words.** `DenseTranscript.statusLabel`: "In progress", "Completed", "Failed", "Declined", "Unfinished" (`stopped`), "Unknown"; any other status is drawn as the runtime wrote it. "Unfinished" and not "Stopped": the turn was stopped, and nothing recorded says what became of the step. "In progress" and not "Running": Claude Code opens a tool item when the model names the tool, before its arguments are complete or its permission is answered.
  - **Normal density is a projection too.** `DenseTranscript.plain(items:turns:)` gives every item in the order given, settled, with each turn's note after that turn's last item; a turn with no items keeps its note at the end. Items with no turn follow dense's grouping (the group of the item before them), so a history step is settled by the turn it stands in. `TranscriptContent` draws `plain` or `entries` through one loop.
  - **Not done.** The coordinator still stores `inProgress`; closing items there would have to choose an outcome nothing reported, and the hub and the phone's mirror would carry it. A turn that ends with no outcome still draws "Turn failed.".
  - **Verification.** `PlainTranscriptTests` (eleven cases), `DenseTranscriptTests.aTurnWhoseOutcomeIsUnknownCounts` (now `unknown`), `TranscriptCorpusTests` F11, F12 and F13; captures `docs/screenshots/transcript-stopped-turn-before.png` and `-after.png`. The dense captures that do not open a turn are byte-identical to the baseline's. Not checked: a real stopped turn on any runtime, the phone, VoiceOver.
