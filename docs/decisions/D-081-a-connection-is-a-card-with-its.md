- **D-081 A connection is a card with its state as an icon and its usage beneath; checks retry.** At the owner's direction ("see which connections are active and what their usage is … pills, loading-indicator style usage, time remaining"; card option B).
  - State is one 16 pt icon: an ink check (connected), a cobalt spinner (checking), a danger "!" (needs you: signed out, unreachable after retries, an update needed, or the plan refusing use), a ghost ring (not checked). The words are the accessibility label and hover help; the line under the name says why it needs you in a few words.
  - Codex and Claude show the plan's windows ("5-hour", "Weekly") as a thin bar with the percent and "Resets in 2h 14m" (or a weekday and time a day or more away, "Reset" once passed); a window at 90% or more is red. A plan with one window shows it at the leading edge (both of the owner's live accounts report only a weekly window). Claude adds the reading's age. B4 shows tokens used today and this week (Monday start, local time).
  - Adding is a "+" menu in the Settings heading: Codex and Claude go straight to sign-in, B4 opens its form; "Re-check runtimes" is gone. The page checks every connection when it opens, except one with a turn in progress.
  - A check that finds the runtime unreachable (`offline`, or `endpoint_unavailable`) tries again after 1 s and 3 s, announcing each try on `connection.changed` (`attempt`, `attempts`) so the card reads "Checking… try 2 of 3". A missing executable, a sign-in state, or an incompatible route is an answer and is not retried.
