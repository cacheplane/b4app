## 2026-11-14 (picker components)

- **D-227 The command palette, the composer's `/` list and the folder picker are drawn from one set of parts (amends D-207 and D-222).** At the owner's direction, after asking why the `/` list looked different from the palette: "Yes, bring the slash list in line, match them. I think the folder selection list should also match. Can we build reusable components for these?"
  - **Why they differed.** The three were written at different times as three views. They shared the command catalog, the selection highlight (D-222) and the keycaps (D-221), and each drew its own card, row and footer: three corner radii (18, 12 and 14 pt), two row heights, a footer of bordered buttons in the folder picker, no symbols, notes or headings in the `/` list, and a `/` list the pointer could not select in.
  - **The parts.** `Sources/WorkspaceUI/Theme/PickerList.swift`: `PickerMetrics` (a 32 pt row, 8 pt inset, 28 pt heading, 40 pt footer, 36 pt field, 18 pt circular corners), `pickerCard`, `PickerSectionTitle`, `PickerRow` (lead, title, note, then value, "Current", shortcut caps and chevron, on the shared highlight), `PickerSymbol`, `PickerFooter`, `PickerHint`, and `pickerHover` (the pointer selects with the keys' glide, and only when it moved). Each list keeps its own model and its own keys; the parts only draw. The phone's palette sheet and place sheet are system lists and are unchanged.
  - **The `/` list.** Rows gain the command's symbol, the palette's title (with its ellipsis) and note, and the palette's headings while nothing is typed: the thread's title or "New thread", "Workspace", and the runtime's name; a typed token is one plain list, as a typed palette query is. A command's values are listed under its name, where a badge was. The pointer now selects. The typed name in the mono face stays, as the one thing this list has that the palette does not: it is what is being typed. The footer's primary action is now a button. On the phone the row keeps its 44 pt height.
  - **The folder picker.** The field is the palette's (the magnifier, the body face, the esc cap, 36 pt), rows are 32 pt with a folder symbol, the path in the mono face at the trailing edge, and "Current" with a check where a leading check was. The footer is the palette's: "No folder", "Choose folder…" with Command and O in caps, and "Start here" with Return; the bordered buttons, the raised footer and "esc to close" are gone. Its keys and its 420 pt width are unchanged.
  - **Not changed.** No key binding, no command, no order of rows, and nothing in how each list opens or closes.
  - **Checked.** The snapshot suite's `palette-*`, `slash` and `placePalette` pages in both appearances, by eye; `SlashControllerTests.commandsAreListedUnderThePalettesHeadingsUntilATokenNarrowsThem`. Not yet looked at in the running app, nor with VoiceOver.
