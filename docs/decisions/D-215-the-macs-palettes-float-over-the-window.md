## 2026-11-12 (floating palettes)

- **D-215 On the Mac the command palette and the place palette are panels over the window, not views inside it (amends D-207 and D-209).** The owner found both "inside" the app: the place palette was cut when the window was at its minimum height, and asked that the command palette could be dragged and reach past the window.
  - **`FloatingPanel`** (`Sources/WorkspaceUI/Theme/FloatingPanel.swift`, the Mac only). A view presents its content in a borderless, non-activating `NSPanel` that can take the keyboard but never becomes the main window. The panel is a child of the window, so it moves with it and follows it to full screen, and it is not clipped by it. It is transparent and as large as the content at its tallest plus a 48 pt margin for the shadow, with the content top-aligned, so a list that grows and shrinks never moves or resizes the panel. It opens centred across the view that presents it, is kept on the screen, fades in over 120 ms (60 ms with Reduce Motion), and leaves with its presentation or with the view. Losing the keyboard (a click on the window, another app) dismisses it; the window takes the keyboard back.
  - **Command palette.** `WorkspaceRootView` presents `PaletteView(floating:)`: the card alone, its top 96 pt under the window's top as before. The scrim over the window is gone; the card's own shadow sets it apart. The header's and footer's empty stretches drag it (`WindowDragArea`); the field and the rows keep their clicks. It opens where it was left, relative to the window, until the app quits.
  - **Place palette.** The veil and the blur stay in the new-thread view, and a click on the veil still closes; the palette itself is in a panel centred over that view, so a short window no longer cuts it. It is not draggable: it belongs to the composer under it. While its folder panel is open the palette stays, and takes the keyboard back when the panel closes.
  - **Composer's `/` list.** Still inside the window. In the new-thread view it now goes above the field when there is no room for it below.
  - **Without panels.** The environment value `floatsPalettes` is set by the app's root; a view drawn without it (the snapshot suite's shell, a preview) keeps both palettes inside itself, as before. The iPhone is unchanged.
  - **Tests.** `FloatingPanelTests` drive a real window and panel: it opens centred with its top where asked, reaches below a window too short for it, closes with its presentation and with its view, and reopens where a drag left it. The snapshot suite draws the card as the panel shows it. Not checked: taking and returning the keyboard, dragging by hand, full screen, and a second display, because macOS would not enable UI automation without the owner's password; the key-driven smoke test has not run against the panel.
