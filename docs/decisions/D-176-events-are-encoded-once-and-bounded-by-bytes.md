## 2026-11-04 (the low-priority relay hardening)

The low-priority findings from reading Phoenix's source against the hub (`docs/research/2026-11-03-phoenix-source-hardening/`). Numbered D-176 by `Scripts/docs-index.sh --next` (cacheplane/b4app#112 holds D-175).

- **D-176 Live events are encoded once and bounded by bytes per link; presence is delivered in snapshot order; the in-memory maps forget what they no longer need; the relay reports its load and its drain.**
  - **Events.** A link's outbound queue now carries `Outbound::Message` (encoded by the link task, as before) or `Outbound::Event`, an `Arc<str>` that `Relay::fan_out` encodes once for every watcher rather than cloning the `serde_json::Value` (up to 256 KiB) and re-encoding it per link, as Phoenix's channel fastlane does. Each link counts the bytes of the events waiting in its queue; one that would take them past `EVENT_BYTES_MAX` (4 MiB) is dropped into the thread's `stream_gap`, as when the queue is full, so a phone that stops reading holds 4 MiB rather than 256 events of up to 256 KiB (64 MiB). The link task gives the bytes back as it writes. Dropping events now logs the device once per gap.
  - **Presence.** `broadcast_presence` reads who is online and delivers the frame under one lock, so two broadcasts racing on different threads (a Mac leaving while another links) reach every phone in the order their snapshots were taken; before, the older snapshot could land last and leave a phone showing a Mac offline.
  - **Maps.** `Notifier` drops the orgs nobody waits on when a pull takes its future (a wake for an org with no entry has nobody to wake), and `LastTouched` sweeps the devices not seen for a minute at most once a minute; both grew by every org and device ever seen.
  - **Logs.** About once a minute the heartbeat loop logs `relay load` with the instance's links and users; the drain logs the links it is closing and how long they took.
  - **Tests.** `relay::registry`: events past the byte budget become one gap and written ones give room back (it would queue all five without the budget). `notify`: orgs nobody waits on are forgotten and the kept one is still woken. `auth`: devices not seen for an interval are forgotten and are due again. The presence ordering has no test: forcing two broadcasts to interleave between snapshot and delivery needs a seam the change itself removes.
