## 2026-11-18 (a turn from another device and a reader who scrolled back)

- **D-254 Only a message the reader sends from this app takes the transcript back to the end; a turn started anywhere else leaves a reader who scrolled back where they are. Amends D-223 call 7.** The hill climb's audit read from the code that a new turn started from another device took a reader who had scrolled back to the end of the thread, as a local send does (its defect 15). D-223's call 7 released the hold when the reader "sends a message", and the transcript took any rise in the thread's turn count to be that, because on the Mac it had no other sign of a send.
  - **The sign.** `WorkspaceModel` counts the messages this app sends in each thread (`ownSends(in:)`), at the press, on the Mac's socket path and on the phone's relay path. `TranscriptView` takes the open thread's count as `sent` and, when it rises, lets go of the hold and goes to the end. The count is in memory only and is never synced.
  - **A turn from elsewhere.** A turn another device or the CLI starts is drawn at the end of the thread as before. A reader at the end follows it. A reader who scrolled back, or who holds a turn open, stays put and has "Jump to latest".
  - **The phone.** The phone released on a change of its pending send's id, which also fired when the pending send was replaced by its turn's own message, taking a reader who had scrolled back in between to the end a second time. It now uses the same count, so only the press does it.
  - **Left out.** Telling the reader that a turn arrived below while they read (a count on "Jump to latest", an announcement): the pill already appears whenever the view is held, and what it should say is part of the audit's defect 9.
