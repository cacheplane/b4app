## 2026-10-31 (visual system)

- **D-036 The apps adopt the LiveLoveApp visual system from `~/repos/liveloveapp`: ink on white, one cobalt accent, Hanken Grotesk and JetBrains Mono, hairlines and pills, no gradients, shadows, glass, uppercase labels, or decorative icon sets.** Tokens live in `Sources/WorkspaceUI/Theme/` and views compose them; the dark palette is derived (ink and bg swap, accent lifted to `#7f98e0` for 6.9:1 on ink) because the site is light only. The accent marks links, focus, the mark, and one state: "waiting for you", the thing to click. Other states use ink shades; failures use the site's danger red. The fonts ship inside the `WorkspaceUI` resource bundle under the SIL Open Font License (files and licences in `Resources/Fonts`) and register with CoreText once per process; when a face is missing the system font stands in. Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-lla-visual-system-design.md`.
