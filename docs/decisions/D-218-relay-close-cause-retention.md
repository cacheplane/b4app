## 2026-11-12 (relay close causes)

- **D-218 An ordinary relay link retains its first accepted blocking close through its final removal (amends D-162).** A queued writer can discover that its generation is blocked after logout has already consumed the close notification, returning the replacement fallback 4000 instead of the session-ended 4001. The link's generation now retains the complete first accepted non-Drain close under the existing registration mutex. Drain remains provisional; later causes do not override an earlier accepted blocking cause by priority.
  - **The cutoff.** The four existing close setters and replacement record the cause before notification or replacement. The ordinary committed link publishes its local blocking result before discarding pending frames, then performs the existing last-seen write. One registration-lock operation selects the retained cause and applies the existing host/grace/code/disposition linger predicate while removing only that exact generation and forgetting its watches. A setter accepted before removal participates; a request after removal does not change that result.
  - **Generation ownership.** Cloned handles share their generation's cell; a replacement gets a fresh cell. An old task can use its own retained cause but cannot remove the replacement's entry, watches, linger or ownership. Watch notifications follow lock release. No new lock crosses an await. The early registration-failure, abandon and Drop paths retain their existing behavior.
  - **Scope and evidence.** This records a close reason, not admission authority. Existing native checks, wire codes, queue limits and claim-release order remain. The gap between a successful proof and transport write remains open; this decision does not promise global transport quiescence. The controlled managed logout regression at CI43305207788 reached actual4000 versus required4001 with HTTP204, exact queued generation, same-socket Close/flush, Mac Pong and owned cleanup prerequisites satisfied; its replacement companion passed. Fourteen added unit policy controls and the production correction require fresh managed compilation and native GREEN before qualification. No deployment or release is authorized by this decision.
