## 2026-11-18 (the activity line)

- **D-255 A thread has one transcript, a turn's work is one line that is always drawn while the turn runs, and opening the line draws one row per step, a sub-agent as a row that opens to its own steps (amends D-223, D-239, D-243, D-246 and D-218).** The design is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-18-activity-line-design.md`, the second of the two designs for the transcript (the first is D-252); the owner approved the look as a clickable mockup on 2026-11-18. Before this, a thread was drawn two ways. Normal density drew every item, each tool as a card (D-234, D-235), with a running line after the last item (D-239). Dense drew the message, one steps line and the answer (D-223), and had a running line only before a turn's first item. A sub-agent had no presentation (defect 11 of `docs/research/2026-11-14-agent-chat-hill-climb.md`), and neither a step nor a turn showed how long it took or how long it had run. The owner's words: remove dense from the chat UI, no multiple view options, no single cards ("its noise and big and unnecessary"), and work that is "summarized to a single line" that "clearly indicates that the agent is still working".
  - **One transcript.** `DenseTranscript.plain`, `WorkspaceModel.denseThread`, the `dense` parameter of `TranscriptView`, `ToolCard`, `CollapsibleCard`, `RunningLine` and the `.running` entry are deleted. `DenseTranscript` keeps its name and is the one projection; the design left a rename as a mechanical last step and it was not done. A thread draws the same on the Mac and the phone at either value of the density setting.
  - **The line.** `ActivityLine` is one row per turn, after the user's message and before the turn's messages (D-223 call 9), one line high at ordinary type sizes and cut at its tail; at accessibility sizes it stacks. While a turn runs it is always drawn, with nothing yet to fold, with only a streaming reply, with only a plan, and for a turn with no items at all (begun on another device), where it stands after everything. It carries the working mark (`ThreadMark` in its working state) for a running or stopping turn, the name of the newest in-progress counted step as `Working` when there is none, and a clock (`0:42`, `1:15`, `1:00:00`) counted from the turn's `createdAt`, in a `TimelineView` that holds only the clock's text, so a tick never re-runs the projection (`DenseTranscript.projections(of:)` is the test's counter). A turn waiting for the user reads `Waiting for you` with no mark and no clock, since the request below it is what asks; a stopping turn reads `Stopping`, with the mark and the clock. A turn that has ended reads `4 steps` or `1 step`, then `N failed` (the only danger-coloured words) and `N declined`, then ` · 1m 12s`; D-223's counting, its answer rule, its notes, its `Show working` for a turn with no counted step and its unknown-outcome rule are unchanged. A closed line is a button only when something folds behind it; its spoken form is its words, the clock in words (`1 minute 15 seconds`) and `Show` or `Hide`, with the value `Collapsed` or `Expanded`.
  - **The step rows.** Opening a turn draws, after its line and in sequence order, one row per folded item (`StepRowView`, `Sources/WorkspaceUI/StepRowView.swift`): a mark, a title and a time at the right. The marks are a check (done), the working mark with a clock (running), a still open circle with no clock (a step of a turn waiting for the user), a cross in the danger colour with the title in it (failed), a dash (declined, or left unfinished by a turn that ended), and none for a thought, a message or a plan. A running step has a clock only when it is a counted step or an agent. The row is spoken as its state, its title and its time; a row that opens is a button with the value `Collapsed` or `Expanded`. A step with nothing to show is not a control. A row opens to its output, one level further in, drawn as a quiet block (below). `ToolCard`'s title rules are unchanged (`theStepTitleIsTheCardsWas`). A tool item before a thread's first group, which D-223 call 6 drew as it was, is a step row at depth 1 with no line above it. No row animates when it opens or closes (`withAnimation(.none)`), and opening a row holds the view as opening a turn does (D-223 call 7). `statusLabel("inProgress")` now reads `Running`, not `In progress`, since the spoken state of a row is the design's form; the card badge that read `In progress` is gone.
  - **The output block.** The card's body was kept at first and looked wrong in the captures: a black block in light mode, its text centred by a two-axis scroll view, clipped on the phone, boxless in dark. A step's input, output and diffs now draw in a quiet block (`StepOutputBlock`: the raised surface fill, small mono ink, leading-aligned, sideways scroll only, selectable), cut at 20 lines with `Show all N lines` and `Show fewer lines`. Pressing it holds the view (`holdTranscript`, an environment action set by `TranscriptView`) so the bottom-pinned transcript does not move the pressed row, and the control is as tall as a row. Captions, the working directory, `No text output.`, the exit code, a change's path and the raw-payload fallback are what `ToolCard` drew. Fenced code in a reply is still `CodeFence` (D-236).
  - **Sub-agents.** A sub-agent call is an agent row, titled `Agent: <first line of its description>` and, once it has children, followed by ` · N steps` (direct children; a nested agent is one step of its parent and counts its own on its row). Its children are the items whose `payload.parentNativeItemId` is the call's `nativeItemId`; a tree is built once per projection, so a child is drawn once, a child whose parent is not in the thread stays where it is until the parent arrives, and a parent cycle or a self-parent leaves its items at the top level. Opened, an agent draws its children one level in with their own rule, nested to four levels (deeper children are drawn at the fourth), then its own output after them as a separate entry (`Entry.output`, id `output-<item id>`). That output is the result alone, as a quiet block; the call's input (its description and full prompt as JSON) is not drawn with it. A sub-agent's messages are folded work, never the turn's answer, whatever their place; a closed turn counts its top-level steps only and a child's failure is not the turn's failure (`aFailedChildIsNotTheTurnsFailure`). A running turn's line names a running agent by its title, not the step inside it. A Codex `collabAgentToolCall`, a `Task` and an `Agent` are agent rows; a Codex agent call has no children and no output item, so its title is the first line of its `prompt` (`payload.prompt`, else `input.prompt`) and an agent row with no children and no output opens to its full detail, which is the payload.
  - **What the recording showed (Claude Code 2.1.282, one turn, one sub-agent, 2026-11-18).** The tool is `Agent` (input `description`, `subagent_type`, `prompt`). Every frame the sub-agent sends carries `parent_tool_use_id`, the call's tool use id: its first frame, a `user` frame whose text block is its prompt (already the call's `input.prompt`; it is not a user message row), its tool uses (`assistant` frames only; no `stream_event` frame was sent for the sub-agent), and their results (`user` frames with `tool_result` blocks, out of order: `Read`'s before `Bash`'s). The sub-agent sent no text or thinking frame of its own; its report is the call's own `tool_result`, a `user` frame with a null parent. `system` frames `task_started`, `task_progress`, `task_summary`, `task_updated`, `task_notification` and `post_turn_summary` carry `tool_use_id` for the call and no parent, and produce nothing. `ClaudeEvents` writes `parentNativeItemId` into every item of a frame with a parent (started, completed, failed and declined; a result for a tool use it never saw takes the parent its frame names), never streams such a frame's text, and clears its table when the turn finishes. The recording is `Tests/ClaudeAdapterTests/Fixtures/subagent-run.jsonl`, scrubbed (working directory `/work/proj`, session id `session-1`, request ids `req-N`, signature `sig`, tool-input and final text deltas each one delta, the final answer a fixed sentence); `fake-claude.py` replays it for a message containing `subagent`. The versions are in `docs/compatibility.md`. The Claude Code transcript on disk does not hold a sub-agent's lines, so history never produces a sub-agent's steps.
  - **`endedAt`.** `SessionService` stamps `payload.endedAt` (a timestamp, when the coordinator processed the event, not when the adapter made it) on every `toolCompleted` item, whatever its final status; a repeated completion restamps it. A step row's duration is `endedAt` less the item's `createdAt` (`1s`, also for 0; `8s`, `59s`, `1m`, `1m 12s`, `59m 59s`, `1h`, `1h 4m`), only for a counted step or an agent that is not running or waiting, never negative, and none when `endedAt` is absent or does not parse. It survives a reconcile (`aCompletedToolItemRecordsWhenItEnded`). Both new payload fields are JSON that already syncs: no migration, no hub deploy, no change to the sync fixtures, and the phone gets both.
  - **The turn's duration.** A terminal turn's duration is `updatedAt` less `createdAt`. The design said to use the latest item end and fall back to item times; that is replaced, and the rule was changed three times during review. The first audit found that `updateTurn` sets `updatedAt` on every call, so a late `turnAcknowledged` on an ended turn would move it; `SessionService` now skips that no-op write for a terminal turn (`aLateAcknowledgementLeavesAnEndedTurnAlone`), so `updatedAt` stays the end. A turn ended by a reconcile or an abandon has `updatedAt` equal to when someone next looked, which can be a day later, so the projection gives no duration to a terminal turn whose `submission` is `unknown`. Setting `submission` to `acknowledged` on a live finish would have separated a late acknowledgement from a reconcile, but it would change `mergeSnapshotItems`, which lets a history item take a live row's id only in a terminal acknowledged turn, so the coordinator was left alone. What is left over: a turn whose acknowledgement was late (over 30 s on Claude Code) and which then finished live shows its count and no time, and a duplicate `turnFinished` still moves `updatedAt`, so a duration can read long. A turn that is `reconciling`, a history group and an old turn that does not parse have none.
  - **Differences from the design file, which is not edited.** (1) The duration rule above, in place of the item-derived end. (2) `Mark.waiting` (the still open circle, spoken `Waiting`) for a step in a `waitingForUser` turn, which the design did not draw; submitting, running and stopping turns keep the running mark and the clock, since their own line is marked. (3) The quiet output block with its 20-line cut and `Show all`, where the design said the step's output is "what `ToolCard` draws in its body". (4) An agent's output is its result alone. (5) A Codex agent's title comes from its prompt, with the payload shown when nothing else is, which the design did not say. (6) A sub-agent's result is a separate trailing entry of its own, not part of the agent's row.
  - **The density setting's scope (amends D-218; D-208 and D-213 stand).** The setting is the same synced `density` column of the `viewSettings` row, with the same migrations, and its default is still `normal`; D-208's row and D-213's per-user storage are untouched, and D-218's values, conversions and write rules are unchanged. What changes is what the setting does: D-218 and D-223 had it change the thread on both devices, and it now changes only the Mac's sidebar rows (`tightQuietRows`) and the Mac's composer (`denseComposer`, D-224). The owner's "dense as the default" is read as the transcript's form, which is now the only one; the writer's call, for the owner to correct. The switch's description on both builds is "Tighter rows for the threads that don't need you, and a composer without its pickers." (D-223 call 11's wording is replaced). The phone has no Display page: D-223 gave the phone one because dense drew a thread there, and the switch would now do nothing on the phone (its rows are 44 pt at either value and its composer keeps its pickers). `SettingsPage.display` stays in the enum, and the phone's page list is `[.account, .connections, .servers, .diagnostics]`; nothing on the phone reaches `DisplaySettingsView`, which still compiles on iOS. A density a phone already holds is read as before and does nothing there.
  - **What amends what.** D-223: its dense projection is the transcript; calls 1 to 5 and 7 to 10 stand (naming, the answer, counting, declined, settling, reconciling, the order, scroll), the steps line gains the mark and the clock, the opened turn is rows and not cards, and call 11 is replaced. D-239: the running line is no longer a row of its own, normal density's line after the last item is gone, and dense's line before the first item is the same line; `Stopping` is now a word. D-243: a plan is drawn after the turn's line while it runs and folds with the other working rows once the turn ends, as before, and a running turn with only a list now has the line, where D-243 gave it no running line. D-246: the rule that a running turn with a list has a running line unless a step is in progress is replaced by "a running turn always has its line". D-234 and D-235's card-specific words (a card's badge, a card's body) describe what is now a row; their statuses and outcomes are unchanged.
  - **Calls made without the owner.** Each is the controller's or the writer's, made in the review of the build, and what it costs if wrong:
    - *A running turn with no items draws its line after everything.* The spec said a running turn has the line from the moment it exists. If wrong, one test flips.
    - *A tool or reasoning item before a thread's first group is a step row at depth 1.* The spec says no cards anywhere. If wrong, a stray row is indented one level.
    - *One tick of the clock is enough for the hosted test that the projection does not re-run (about 1.2 s).* A slower test buys nothing.
    - *The ended turn's duration is `updatedAt` less `createdAt`, with none for a turn whose submission is unknown.* Changed during review, from the design's item-derived end, then to an unconditional `updatedAt`, then to this, because item times are starts and read "1s" for a one-item reply, and because a reconcile's `updatedAt` is the time someone looked. The coordinator's `submission` was not changed, because `mergeSnapshotItems` reads it. If wrong, a turn shows no time where a true one existed, or one reads long after a duplicate `turnFinished`.
    - *`Mark.waiting` for a step in a waiting turn, and `Running` for one in a stopping turn.* If wrong, a stopping turn's open step turns for the seconds it takes to stop.
    - *The output is a quiet block with a 20-line cut, and an agent's output is its result alone.* The design named the content and the mockup the look; the build kept the card's look. If wrong, one extra task's cost, and the cut changes the limit in one constant.
    - *An agent row with no children and no output opens to its full detail, and its title falls back to the first line of its prompt.* The Codex payload shape is believed, not recorded; if wrong, a Codex agent row opens to raw JSON that reads poorly. Narrowed in review: an agent with children and no output gets no trailing entry, so a running sub-agent's prompt does not appear under its steps; if wrong, a Claude sub-agent whose result never came hides its prompt.
    - *A child settles with the turn of the row it stands under.* A child whose own turn has ended, and whose parent is in a running turn, reads running. If wrong, a step is shown running a little longer than it was.
    - *A window cut inside an opened turn or agent is accepted.* The 150-row window (D-237) can cut between an agent row and its children, or hide an opened turn's line, and pressing `Show` on a turn with more than about 150 rows can push the pressed line out of the drawn window. Real, deferred.
    - *The phone's Display page is removed and the default stays `normal`.* If wrong, a Display page comes back with one switch that does nothing there, or the owner's default is changed in one place (`ViewSettings`).
    - *The whole-branch review and its fixes ran before these records,* so the decision and the gate record are written once against the final code. Last time the records needed a correction pass after a fix wave.
  - **Not done.** Announcing a change to VoiceOver (defect 9). The failed step's name or reason on the closed line. Command output while a command runs. Durations for steps read from history, and any sub-agent steps from history. Syntax colouring in output. Renaming `DenseTranscript`. Streaming of a sub-agent's text.
  - **Old data.** A thread from before this has neither field: its sub-agent steps stay flat, among the turn's own steps, and its steps show no time; a turn already finished keeps whatever `updatedAt` it had, so its duration is what that was. A thread rebuilt only from history shows the agent call with its result and no children.
